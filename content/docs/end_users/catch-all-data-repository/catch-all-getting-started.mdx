title: Getting Access to the Data Catch-all Repository
---

This guide explains how to access public records and obtain permission to
create records in the [Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

## Browsing Public Records

Visit the [Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

No registration is required to:

- search for records using the search field;
- select **Browse all records** to browse published records;
- view publicly available metadata;
- download publicly accessible files.

![Public search page of the Data Catch-all Repository](/img/nrp/catch-all-data-repository/data-repo1.png)

## Getting Permission to Create Records

To create records, you need deposit permission, which you can obtain in one
of two ways:

- **through a community** — recommended, as your data then gets a curator and
  a place next to related datasets; or
- **as an individual depositor**, if no suitable community exists for your
  data.

Deposition is open primarily to employees and students of eligible academic
and research institutions in the Czech Republic, their international
collaborators, and other approved users whose intended use is consistent with
the mission of the repository.

Both paths are managed on the repository's
[Get access page](https://datarepo.eosc.cz/get-access). When you are signed
in without deposit permission, the **+** button in the top navigation and the
**Want to deposit your data?** card on the front page both lead there.

![The Get access page with both access paths](/img/nrp/catch-all-data-repository/get-access-page.png)

### Getting Access Through a Community

On the [Get access page](https://datarepo.eosc.cz/get-access):

- **Open membership communities**: select **Find a community**, navigate to
  the community page, and select **Request Membership**. Fill in the form,
  stating your reason for joining. You will receive an email once your
  membership is approved, and you can track the status of your request on
  your dashboard under **Requests**.
- **Closed membership communities** do not accept online applications —
  contact the community manager directly. You can find a contact on the
  community's About page, or write to
  [datarepo@eosc.cz](mailto:datarepo@eosc.cz).

For more information about communities and community roles, see the
[Guide for Communities](guide-for-communities).

### Becoming an Individual Depositor

If no suitable community exists for your data, apply on the
[Get access page](https://datarepo.eosc.cz/get-access) as an individual
depositor:

1. In the **As an individual depositor** panel, submit the application form
   using the **Submitters** button. You may be asked to describe your
   intended use of the repository.

2. Wait for review. Applications are evaluated manually, so approval may not
   be immediate — you will receive an email once your application has been
   processed.

3. **Sign out and sign in again** after your application has been approved.
   Your new permissions are only loaded on a fresh sign-in — if you remain
   signed in with an older session, the repository will keep directing you
   to the Get access page instead of letting you deposit.

4. [Deposit your first dataset](catch-all-repository-record-creation).

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
If you still cannot create records after signing out and signing back in,
contact the repository support team at
[datarepo@eosc.cz](mailto:datarepo@eosc.cz) and mention the approval
notification you received.
</Callout>

## Signing in to the Repository

Browse to the
[Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

Select **Log in** and choose your home institution.

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
Signing in does not automatically grant permission to create records.
Deposit permission requires the **Submitters** role or a community role that
permits record submission.
</Callout>

After signing in, you can search and browse public records in the same way as
users who are not signed in. Depending on your permissions, you can also
create records, manage drafts, and access community functions.

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
Select **My dashboard** to access your drafts and manage your records.
</Callout>
