---
title: Getting Access to the Data Catch-all Repository
---

This guide explains how to access public records and obtain permission to
create records in the [Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

## Browsing Public Records

Visit the [Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

No registration is required to:

- search for records using the search field;
- select **Browse all records** to browse published records;
- view publicly available metadata;
- download publicly accessible files.

![Public search page of the Data Catch-all Repository](/img/nrp/catch-all-data-repository/data-repo1.png)

## Getting Permission to Create Records

To create records, you must obtain write access either:

- as an individual submitter, referred to as a standalone depositor; or
- through a community role that permits record submission.

Write access is intended primarily for employees and students of eligible
academic and research institutions in the Czech Republic, their international
collaborators, and other approved users whose intended use is consistent with
the mission of the repository.

During the application process, you may be asked to describe your intended use
of the repository and justify your request for access.

### Becoming a Standalone Depositor

Follow this procedure if you want to obtain write access as an individual
rather than through a dedicated community.

Write access for standalone depositors is managed through the
**Submitters** group in the **VO_repo_datarepo_prod** virtual organisation.
These names may appear during the application process or in communication
with user support.

To apply:

1. Submit the
   [application form](https://signup.e-infra.cz/allfed/registrar/?vo=VO_repo_datarepo_prod&group=repository:roles:Submitters).

2. Wait for your application to be reviewed. Applications may require manual
   evaluation, so approval may not be immediate. You will receive an email
   notification when the application has been processed.

3. After your application is approved, sign in to the
   [Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz/)
   and
   [create your first record](catch-all-repository-record-creation).

### Getting Access Through a Community

Access to an existing community is normally provided through an invitation
from a **Community owner** in your team, project, or organisation.

The invitation assigns you one of the available community roles. The assigned
role determines which records and community actions are available to you.

Not every community role grants permission to submit records. To create
records through a community, you must be assigned a role that permits record
submission.

If you do not know who the Community owner is, contact the repository support
team at
[datarepo@eosc.cz](mailto:datarepo@eosc.cz).

For more information about communities and community roles, see the
[Guide for Communities](guide-for-communities).

## Signing in to the Repository

Browse to the
[Data Catch-all Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

Select **Log in** and choose your home institution.

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
Signing in does not automatically grant permission to create records. Write
access requires membership in the **Submitters** group or a community role
that permits record submission.
</Callout>

After signing in, you can search and browse public records in the same way as
users who are not signed in. Depending on your permissions, you can also
create records, manage drafts, and access community functions.

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
Select **My dashboard** to access your drafts and manage your records.
</Callout>
