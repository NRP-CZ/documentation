This guide describes how to get access to the [Catch-all Data
Repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

## Reading Records in the Repository: Public Access

Visit the [catch-all repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

Any member of the public (no registration needed) can
- search for keywords using the Search field
- or browse all published records in the catch-all data repository by using **BROWSE ALL RECORDS**
- data and metadata are provided with no guarantees; the repository and its operator assume no liability for any issues arising from their use

## Creating Records in the Repository: Registration Needed

To deposit data into the repository, you either apply for write access to the repository as an individual (“standalone depositor”), or you need to be a member of a community within the repository
(if depositing on behalf of that community). In other words, you need to
register.

Membership is available primarily to employees and students of academic institutions in the Czech Republic, their international collaborators, and also members of the general public who intend to publish their scientifically interesting datasets. Kindly note that you will be asked about your usage intentions and justification of your membership.

### Becoming a Standalone Depositor

If you want to gain write access to the repository as an individual (i.e.
not in a community).

Write access to the repository is managed by the “**Submitters** group in the **VO_repo_datarepo_prod** virtual organisation”. Do not let it scare you, it is useful to know these keywords in the process (or when requesting support).

To apply:

1. You need to submit the **[application form](https://signup.e-infra.cz/allfed/registrar/?vo=VO_repo_datarepo_prod&group=repository:roles:Submitters)**.

2. Once your application is approved you will receive a mail notification.
   Your application may be evaluated manually, so expect a reasonable
   delay.

3. Then you can continue to **[Catch-all data repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz/)** and log in, which gives you permissions to **[create your first dataset](catch-all-repository-record-creation)**.

### Becoming a Community Depositor

To become a community depositor, the registration process will be described to you by the community administrator in your team/project/organization, most likely in the form of an invitation e-mail.

If you do not know the community administrator, contact our support team at 
[datarepo@eosc.cz](mailto:datarepo@eosc.cz).

To learn more about communities (and even how to establish a community),
consult the [community guide](guide-for-communities).

## Logging into the Repository

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
You need to be registered as described above to be able to log into the repository.
</Callout>

Browse to the [catch-all repository](https://datarepo.eosc.cz).

To create and manipulate your records and/or to handle communities, you need to log in via the **Log in** button and select your home institution.

Naturally, authenticated users can search for records and/or **BROWSE ALL RECORDS**
in the repository the same way as the general public.

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
If you click on **BROWSE ALL RECORDS** you can see all public records. To see your drafts you have to **select the community** where you drafted your record. That is why the number of **results** can differ depending on communities selected.
</Callout>
