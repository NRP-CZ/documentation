---
title: Obtaining Write Access to the National Metadata Directory
---

While the majority of metadata records should be automatically harvested to the
National Metadata Directory (NMD), it may be necessary to create a metadata
record for datasets from repositories that are not yet harvested by the NMD
automatically. To do so, you need a user account.

Note that getting user account is not needed just to browse and/or search records.

## Getting User Account in the NMD

Anybody can get a user account in the NMD. If you are affiliated with an
academic institution in the Czech Republic (e.g. a university student or
employee, AS ČR employee, ...), an automatic registration is all you need.
Otherwise, your application will be decided upon automatically.

Browse to https://nma.eosc.cz. Select **Sign in**.

<Callout type="warn" title="TESTING">
  Web interface for internal record ingest testing is https://test1.nma.du.cesnet.cz/
</Callout>


You will be presented with a list of institutions. If you are affiliated
with an academic institution in the Czech Republic, you should find it in
the list, such as in this example:
![sign-in-with-social-identities](/img/nrp/national-metadata-directory/institutional-sign-in.png)
Using your institutional login in highly recommended.

If it is not the case, you may use your identity from one of following companies: Apple, GitHub, Google, LinkedIn, Microsoft, and/or ORCID. Just search for the company name in the institution selection box:
![sign-in-with-social-identities](/img/nrp/national-metadata-directory/sign-in-with-social-identities.png)

After selecting your institution (or other identity provider), log into
your institution's system. You may be presented with a list of attributes
about your person that are requested by the NMD. Please note that at least
your name and e-mail address are mandatory to get access to the NMD. Should
this be your first encounter with the national e-infrastructure, you may
also be shown formal documents such as personal information processing
statements, asked to verify your email etc.

When using our institutional login, provided you are an employee or a
student of an academic institution, your access will be automatically
approved. Otherwise your application will be checked manually
and you will be notified via email.

## Logging in to the NMD

After obtaining the user account, you can log into the NMD, using the Log
in button. Similar to the registration process, select your institution
from the list (or the social network identity used previously). Your
browser will likely remember the previously selected organisation.

When logged in, you find your username in the upper right corner, you can
also modify your preferences there.
