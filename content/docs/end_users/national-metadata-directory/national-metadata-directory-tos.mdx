title: National Metadata Directory Terms of Service
---

The National Metadata Directory (NMD) is operated
under following Terms of Service:

1. NMD is a service harvesting metadata records from selected repositories
   and allowing users to search for datasets described by metadata records.
1. NMD is a metadata-only repository.
1. NMD is operated by [CESNET](https://www.ces.net/) as a service of the
   National Repository Platform (NRP).
1. Repositories operated in the NRP comply with [terms and conditions for
   operating repostories in the NRP](../../repo_admins/operating-repositories-in-the-nrp/formal-governing-documents).
