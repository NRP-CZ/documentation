The RepoBuilders community connects all roles involved in repository operations – from data curation and methodology to system administration and infrastructure operation. Whether you work with metadata, manage a system, or consult repositories, you will find your place here.​

## About the community​

The RepoBuilders community brings together people who build and operate research data repositories in Czech institutions. Whether you handle technical infrastructure, curate data, or manage a repository – we are creating a space for sharing experience and mutual help.​

The community works on two levels:​
- **General level** - covers topics across repository systems – methodologies, standards and practical procedures
- **System-specific level** - focuses on CESNET Invenio, details on the CESNET Invenio website

We meet online in two formats:
​
- **RepoBuilders meetings** (quarterly) – News from NRP, discussion of current topics, sharing experience across teams. ​
- **CESNET Invenio RepoBuilders meetings** (monthly) – Discussion of CESNET Invenio platform specific topics.​

## Channels​

Stay in touch through these channels:​

- [**Discord**](https://discord.com/channels/1070628199983235143/1446616100115582986) – informal discussion, quick questions and sharing knowledge and experience within the RepoBuilders. An informal space that grew out of the National Repository Platform (NRP) and related EOSC CZ activities. ​
	- #notification-repo-builders – community news, meeting dates and important updates​
	- #materials-for-repo-builders – templates, methodologies, meeting notes and links​
	- #general-repo-builders – open community chat​
	- #ask-for-help-repo-builders – questions and help, no question too small​
	- #invenio – discussion and support for CESNET Invenio system​
	- Private channel: #pilot-repo-builders – pilot group coordination​

Missing a channel for your topic? Let us know in #general.​
