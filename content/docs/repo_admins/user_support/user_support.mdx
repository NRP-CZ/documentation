**If you are an end-user seeking support** regarding a specific repository
or service, there are support contacts on the repository page. If you are
not sure, just contact support@eosc.cz and don't worry.

## Triaging Requests for Repository Administrators

This document gives an overview of user support channels for the NRP. It is
intended mainly for repository adminstrators and service operators, so that
they can reach the necessary service efficiently. Even for those types of
users, contacting support@eosc.cz is completely fine.

Majority of the groups providing support use RT, making it especially easy
to transfer tickets to colleagues who are better equipped to solve them.
We provide a list of NRP-relevant supporting channels.


When in doubt, transfer the ticket to `eosc_cz-support` (which is the queue
behind support@eosc.cz), they should know what to do.
