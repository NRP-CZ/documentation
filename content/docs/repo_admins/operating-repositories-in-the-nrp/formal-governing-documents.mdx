---
title: Formal Documents Governing NRP Operations
---

This document lists formal documents that govern establishment and
operations of repositories and services in the National Repository Platform
(NRP).

It is intended for reference of (future) repository administrators as a
collection of relevant resources.

## Overview of Procedures and Principles

Kindly refer to [the description of establishing repositories in the NRP as
a service](https://www.eosc.cz/en/services/data-storage/repositories-in-nrp) for basic understanding of the principles.

We then recommend getting familiar with [Conditions for Creating New and Modifying Existing Thematic Repositories in the National Repository Platform](conditions-for-creating-repositories). It gives an overview of necessary roles and responsibilities when running a repository in the NRP.

## Formal Documents


Main formally binding documents governing the operation of the National
Repository Platform are the following:
- [Terms and Conditions for operating repositories in the NPR (valid from 23 March 2026)](/docs/governing-documents/P1_PP_repozitare_NRP-20260323.pdf) (in Czech)
  - history of older versions of the document will be kept here
- [Contract Proposal between the Repository Administrator and the Infrastructure](/docs/governing-documents/Smlouva-zrizeni-repozitare_NRP.docx) (current version, in Czech)
- cybersecurity of the infrastructure is bound by [principles described by CESNET (in Czech)](https://www.cesnet.cz/bezpecnost) and also [available in English](https://www.cesnet.cz/en/cybersecurity).

If you need any consultation regarding legal aspects of the NRP, kindly
contact [legal@eosc.cz](mailto:legal@eosc.cz).
